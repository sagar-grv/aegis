# VeilTrace — 2-Minute Demo Script

## Setup

Open the deployed VeilTrace site at desktop width. Begin with the initial **Low Visibility** state. The visual walkthrough included in the submission shows the same three states; this script supports a narrated replacement or extended recording.

| Time | Action | Suggested narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Pause on the hero, Evidence Ledger, Relationship Graph, and Decision Receipt. | “VeilTrace is an explainable financial-fraud intelligence platform for STAMPERS Track 05. It catches coordinated fraud where every individual transaction looks legitimate.” |
| 0:15–0:34 | Point to the baseline score of 41 and the low Camouflage Index. | “At first, the payments are individually normal: modest amounts and plausible merchants. Conventional rules might allow them. VeilTrace treats this as low visibility, not as proof of safety.” |
| 0:34–0:57 | Click **Unmask pattern**. | “When we expand relationship evidence, VeilTrace sees three accounts sharing one device relay, a repeated timing heartbeat, and beneficiary convergence. The Camouflage Index rises to 91, and the recommendation becomes Hold and review.” |
| 0:57–1:18 | Point to the graph and the evidence ledger. | “The important point is explainability. The graph shows the coordinated ring, while the ledger spells out why the score changed. The amount alone did not cause the alert.” |
| 1:18–1:40 | Click **Add verified KYC evidence**. | “Fraud systems must also protect legitimate customers. A verified employment relationship explains the shared device. The False-Positive Guard increases, the risk falls, and VeilTrace moves to enhanced monitoring rather than silently clearing the case.” |
| 1:40–2:00 | Pause on the safeguard and human-approval note. | “VeilTrace does not determine guilt or automate customer-impacting action. It provides reviewable evidence, a counterfactual explanation, and a human analyst gate for every consequential decision.” |
